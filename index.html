Frequently asked questions

1. What is Vaulet?

Vaulet helps you and your friends organise shared goals, record contributions, and track your progress together — all in one place.

2. Does Vaulet hold or transfer our money?

No. Vaulet's MVP doesn't hold or transfer money. You make payments separately and use Vaulet to keep track of recorded contributions.

3. Can I create a vault with my friends?

Absolutely! Create a shared vault, set a target amount and deadline, and invite your friends to work toward the same goal.

4. Are contributions automatically verified?

No. Contributions recorded in Vaulet aren't automatically verified against actual payments. Members should confirm payments separately to keep the records accurate.
